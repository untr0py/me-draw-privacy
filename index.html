me draw privacy policy

me draw works offline, requires no account, and has no ads or analytics. The app collects no user data and does not send your drawings or other information to the developer.

Updated 16 October 2026

Your drawings

Drawings and settings are saved on your device. Delete drawings in the app; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data. Exported PNGs are separate copies and are not removed when you delete drawings or uninstall the app.

Device backups

Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings; the developer does not receive these backups.

Contact

Support and privacy: untropy8421@gmail.com.

If you email the developer, your address and message are used to respond and kept only as needed for support. You can request their deletion.
